## Five most significant fixes

1. **Mahindra and Mahindra's "benefit must be in kind" reasoning (q47, k19, k22, q49).**
   - Section 26(2)(f)(ii) of the 2025 Act charges benefits "in cash or in kind or partly in cash and partly in kind".
   - So old q47 ("which waiver is taxed as business income?") had an arguably correct distractor: both waivers count as business benefits.
   - q47 now asks only which waiver the deemed profit rule for remission catches. The test is whether a deduction was allowed earlier (s.38(1)(a) and (2)(a)), which is the limb the notes carry forward to the 2025 Act.
   - k19 is reframed on that limb and warns that the "in kind" reasoning does not fit the 2025 Act.
   - k22 no longer says "benefits in kind".
   - I removed q49's contrast with a waived loan.
2. **Compulsory depreciation (q30).**
   - The old answer said depreciation is "allowed anyway" for a loss making company.
   - Section 33(11)(a)(ii) allows no depreciation in a year whose profit before depreciation is a loss; the whole amount is carried forward. Section 41(1)(c) reduces written down value only by depreciation "actually allowed".
   - I rewrote q30 so that profits are below the depreciation: Rs. 2 lakh is allowed under s.33(11)(a)(i) and Rs. 3 lakh is carried forward without time limit.
3. **Numbers to recall in correct answers.**
   - q16 ("spread over five years") now says "in equal parts over a set period".
   - q51 ("for the next five years") now states the five year lock-out in the stem and tests the books and audit consequence.
4. **Answers given away by the stem or option form.**

## Inaccuracies flagged: notes against the Act

I followed the notes throughout. No item depends on any of these points.

- **(a) s126, "or the actual profit if higher".**
  - This overstates the rule. The s.58(2) table says "profit claimed to have been actually earned, whichever is higher": the assessee may declare more, but the Assessing Officer does not substitute actual profit.
  - q50 and k30 say only that the assessee "may declare" a higher profit.
  - Suggested note fix: "or a higher profit claimed by the assessee".
- **(b) s117 and s120, Taj Mahal and plant.**
  - Section 66(17) excludes "furniture and fittings" from plant. Taj Mahal's holding that sanitary fittings are plant came before that exclusion and is doubtful today, though its functional definition of plant still holds.
  - No item tests fittings. q61 and k21 use only the functional test (Scientific Engineering House).
- **(c) s111, Piara Singh.**
  - Section 34(2)(a) and (3) bar *expenditure* for an offence. Piara Singh allowed a *loss* (confiscated currency). Whether it survives the bar is debatable.
  - No item turns on it. k20 keeps the notes' "may be allowed".
- **(d) s127 and the exemption limit.**
  - The notes faithfully mirror an inconsistency inside the Act. Section 58(3) requires books and audit only if total income exceeds the exemption limit; s.62(2)(c) and entry 2 of the s.63 table have no such condition.
  - q51 states that income exceeds the limit, so all three provisions agree.
- **(e) New: s109(f) and s125, Mahindra and Mahindra.**
  - The notes give the "benefit must be in kind, not cash" reasoning, but the 2025 Act's benefit clause, s.26(2)(f)(ii), expressly covers benefits "in cash or in kind". This appears to reflect a later amendment of s.28(iv) of the 1961 Act.
  - Suggested note fix: add "in cash or in kind" to item (f) and limit the Mahindra and Mahindra point to s.38(1)(a).
  - Whether waiver of a loan taken to buy capital assets is now caught by s.26(2)(f) is contested. No item depends on it.
- **(f) New, minor: s119, unabsorbed depreciation.**
  - The summary is correct, but it does not say that in a loss year no depreciation is allowed at all (s.33(11)(a)(ii)).
  - q30 avoids that case.
- **(g) New, minor: s109, hedging.**
  - The notes say "exceptions such as hedging". Section 66(31)(b) is narrower: contracts for raw materials or merchandise, made to guard against price changes affecting the assessee's contracts to deliver goods it manufactures or sells.
  - q04's stem now matches this.
