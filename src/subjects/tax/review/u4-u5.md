## Inaccuracies in the notes (flagged; no item depends on them)

- **(a) Sheila Kaushish (s103).** The case summary says "the annual value cannot exceed the standard rent". More precisely, the standard rent caps the *expected* rent under s21(1)(a); actual rent above the standard rent is still annual value under s21(1)(b). The sentence just before the case in s103 is accurate.
  - u5-q12, u5-k05 and u5-k07 use the expected-rent wording.
  - In u5-q12 the actual rent is below the standard rent.
  - **Suggest correcting the slide.**
- **(b) Stock in trade (s98 against s104).** s98 says "two years after completion". The Act's s21(5) says "two years from the end of the financial year in which the certificate for completion of construction is obtained", as s104 states. **Suggest correcting s98.** u5-q19 is neutral.
- **(c) Spouse's remuneration (s84).** The notes state the "spouse whose total income is greater" rule (s99(5)(a)(i)) without the 1961 Act's condition (Explanation 1 to s64(1)(ii)) that *both* spouses have a substantial interest and both receive remuneration. The printed 2025 Act also omits that condition. So the notes follow the Act, but the rule's scope is contested. No item asks which spouse is taxed.
- **(d) East India Housing (s80).** The summary says "whatever the object of the company". That is broader than Chennai Properties (s102) allows where letting is the main business. u4-q03 now makes clear that letting is not the company's business.
- **Points to confirm** (the items avoid depending on them):
  - The unrealised rent conditions come from the old Rule 4, and the 2026 Rule has not been confirmed (u5-q15).
  - The notes do not say whether a house property loss under the new regime can be carried forward, given s202(3) (u5-q34).
  - On a self occupied house, s202(2)(a)(v) as printed withdraws only s22(1)(b), current interest. It does not list s22(1)(c), the pre construction instalments, while the notes say no interest deduction is allowed at all. No quiz item turns on this; u5-k10 follows the notes. u5-q32 concerns a let out flat.
- **Checked and consistent with the Act:**
  - s21(6): "cannot actually occupy it due to any reason" (u5-q16);
  - s195: 30%, with no deduction or set off; no item uses the rate;
  - s15(1) and (3): due or receipt, and advance salary not taxed again;
  - s97(2) and (3): irrevocable trusts;
  - s19(2)(e): voluntary retirement deduction once only;
  - s202(2)(a)(iv): only tax on employment is withdrawn from the salary Table.

## Validation (node)

The file parses as valid JSON and is written as `JSON.stringify(d, null, 2)` plus a final newline, matching the original format. The following checks all pass:

- **Quiz fields:** every item has exactly `{id, u, concept, slide, q, ok, no[3], why}`.
- **Options:** `no` holds 3 unique strings, none equal to `ok`.
- **Ids:** unique across quiz items and cards.
- **Slides:** every slide id exists in `study-data.json` and belongs to the item's unit.
- **Units:** every `u` is "u4" or "u5".
- **Concept labels:** 8 words or fewer.
- **Explanations:** 2 to 4 sentences, each ending with an Authority line.
- **Card backs:** 60 words or fewer.
- **Case cards:** each ends with the case as authority.
- **Style:** no straight apostrophes, hyphens, or HTML in quiz fields.
- **Arithmetic:** every computed answer and every wrong figure was checked in node.

Concept tags often state the rule. That is harmless while `CONCEPT_UP_FRONT` in `src/features/quiz/engine.ts` is `false`.
